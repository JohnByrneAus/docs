Quick Q&A
Who is it for?

Assessors who meet their monthly Key Performance Indicators (KPIs).

Does it replace my PD?

No. It's in addition to your baseline PD requirements & entitlements.

Does it have to relate to my job?

No. It's your choice, and it may not be directly related to your position.

Why are we doing this?

To reward following the toolkit and keeping orderly, current LMS records.
