## Summary

### Pain points
- **Role and visibility.** Admin feel they "just answer the phones and emails" and **don't know how long marking takes**.
- **LMS status is unreliable.** Submissions show up but **can look unmarked**. Staff put this down to the **system falling short** and a **heavy workload**.
- **Marking data is inconsistent.** **Assessors update the system** themselves, so admin **find it hard to monitor assessments**.
- **Trainers get contacted instead of admin**, so queries go to the wrong people.
- **Staff are unsure of the existing policies and procedures.**
- **Remote areas have no LMS access** and work in **offline batch mode**.

### Ideas and requests
- **People:** more trainers/assessors, plus an **extra staff member** (classroom mentor / SSO).
- **Process:**
  - **Assessors update records immediately.**
  - Agree a clear **escalation contact**.
- **Students:** **notify students** about their assessment status, for **transparency**.
- **LMS:** **status controls and training** (heavily emphasised!!!!!).
- **Workflow:** there has been **"talk of" an automated assessment workflow**. Staff would rather have **messaging**, which means **less siloing**.
- **Continuous improvement (CI):** **monthly reports** and **toolbox talks**.
- **Quality:** **work closely with the QA team** to build a **"single source of truth"**.
- **Connectivity:** maybe **Skylink** (possibly Starlink?) for remote areas or data points.

## Possible follow-ups
- [ ] Set up LMS status controls (e.g. submitted / in marking / marked) and train all staff on them.
- [ ] Agree a rule that assessors update records immediately after marking.
- [ ] Name an escalation contact and tell students and trainers who it is.
- [ ] Re-share and confirm the current policies and procedures.
- [ ] Explore messaging-based workflow options as an alternative to full automation.
- [ ] Start monthly reporting and toolbox talks as part of CI.
- [ ] Look into satellite internet for remote sites so they can stop using offline batch mode.
