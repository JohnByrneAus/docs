ACTION PLAN

Assessors + Admin Team: uniform LMS results

Root causeTrainers don't use a uniform practice to update the LMS with student results
4 Actions

1Admin + Assessor Taskforce

Work togetherBetter communication between departments, continual improvement, and it builds the toolkit

Make it work

  • A co-lead from each team; add a QA rep
  • Protected time: meetings count as work, not extra
  • Fortnightly to start, then monthly
  • Include a voice from remote / shift staff
  • First jobs: define LMS statuses & decide who owns data entry

2Assessor Toolkit

Uniform practiceFeedback template + consistent assessment & feedback procedure
Put the LMS steps in itWhat each status means, who updates it, within X days, plus the escalation contact

Make it work

  • One home, one version: owner, version no. & review date (no more duplicates or old copies)
  • Pilot with a few assessors, then roll out
  • Keep it short: one page per process
  • Offline/printable copy for remote sites
  • Pair it with refreshed aXcelerate training & onboarding for new trainers

3PD Rewards System (PDRS)

Team hits target→ team chooses additional PD

Make it work

  • Baseline first, then set realistic targets
  • Measure speed + quality: e.g. % results entered in X days and a QA sample against the toolkit
  • Keep required PD (industry currency / VET skills) separate, so rewards are truly extra
  • Adjust targets for offline / remote sites
  • Publish progress monthly so teams can see it

4Recognition

Toolbox talk shout-outsEvery toolbox talk: quick, specific thanks
Awards night: every 6 monthsEffort · Improvement · Excellence: staff feel valued and build industry credibility

Make it work

  • Tie categories to the goals (e.g. most improved LMS accuracy, best toolkit contribution)
  • Peer nominations as well as manager picks
  • Timing that suits shift & remote staff (or stream it)
  • Certificates staff can add to their CV / portfolio

✓How we'll know it's working

  • % of results entered in the LMS within X days
  • Fewer submissions that look unmarked
  • Fewer calls & emails chasing results
  • QA audits: feedback consistent with the toolkit
  • Staff pulse survey: clarity, confidence, feeling valued

!Watch-outs

  • Workload: give time, or uniform practice won't stick
  • Gaming: speed targets without quality checks backfire
  • Free-riding: mix team rewards with individual recognition
  • Change fatigue: show early wins, keep it simple
  • Fairness: remote, shift & new staff included
