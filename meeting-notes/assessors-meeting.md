## Summary by theme

### People and delivery
- **New trainers ("newbies")** need more training and support.
- **Shift workers** are assessed by a workplace assessor, which **takes time** to arrange.
- **Face-to-face (F2F) delivery** is part of the picture.

### Clarity of assessments
- Assessors are **unsure what the assessment task asks for and what feedback is required**.
- They want **templates and guidelines**, especially a **marking guide**.

### Workload
- **Not enough time for marking.**
- **Assessors do data entry** that could sit with **admin**.
- This competes with their other priorities.

### Systems: the LMS
- **LMS issues** with both **access and usage**.

### Training
- The **aXcelerate** training was a long time ago ("100 yrs ago").
- Some content may be **duplicated**.
- Some may be an **old version**.

## Possible follow-ups
- [ ] Set up onboarding, training and support (e.g. mentoring) for new trainers.
- [ ] Write a standard assessment template and marking guide that states the feedback expected.
- [ ] Look at moving data entry from assessors to the admin team.
- [ ] Refresh aXcelerate / LMS training, then remove duplicated and outdated material.
- [ ] Look at how workplace assessments for shift workers are scheduled.
- [ ] Log and fix LMS access problems.
